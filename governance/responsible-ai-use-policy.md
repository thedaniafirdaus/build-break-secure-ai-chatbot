# Brightwell Home Ltd — Responsible AI Use Policy

**Version** 1.0 · **Owner** AI Lead · **Approved by** Managing Director · **Effective** 17 November 2026 · **Next review** May 2027
*Brightwell Home Ltd is a fictional company created for a portfolio project.*

**Why this exists.** AI tools save us time, but they can leak data, get facts wrong and treat people unfairly. This policy tells every Brightwell employee and contractor how to use AI safely. It covers any tool that generates text, images, code or decisions, including AI features built into software we already use.

## 1. Approved tools

| Tool | Use it for | Data allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 Copilot (work account) | Drafting, summaries, Excel, Teams meeting notes | Internal data, including customer data already held in Microsoft 365 |
| ChatGPT (company Business workspace) | Marketing copy, ideas, rewriting | Public and internal data only — **no personal data** |
| GitHub Copilot Business | Code suggestions | Code only — no secrets or customer data |
| Adobe Firefly | Product image edits | Our own product photos |
| Customer-service AI agent, fraud scoring, recommendations, CV screening | Only as set up and listed in the AI Use-Case Register | As listed in the register |

**Not approved:** personal or free AI accounts used for work, AI browser extensions, AI note-takers that join calls, and uploading company files to any AI website not listed above.

## 2. Data you must never put into AI

**Never into any AI tool:** card or bank details · passwords, API keys, discount or staff codes · health, disciplinary or grievance information · anything about a person's race, religion, sexuality or other special category data.

**Never into a tool that isn't approved for it (see table):** customer names, emails, addresses or order history · CVs or staff records · salaries · supplier contracts and prices · unpublished financial results · anything marked *Confidential*.

If you're not sure, leave it out or ask first.

## 3. Human oversight

- **You own the output.** Check facts, figures, prices and links before you send or publish anything.
- **AI never makes the final decision about a person.** Hiring, refunds over £50, blocking a customer and disciplinary decisions always need a person.
- **Be open with customers.** Our chatbot says it is an AI and offers a route to a person.
- **Report problems.** If AI gives a harmful or wrong answer, or you put data in by mistake, tell the AI Lead the same day. You won't be blamed for reporting.

## 4. Starting a new AI use

1. **Check the register.** If the use is already listed, follow its controls.
2. **If it's new, email the AI Lead** with what it does, which tool, what data it uses and who it affects.
3. **The AI Lead sets the risk tier** within 5 working days:
   - **Low** — no personal data, internal use only, a person checks all output → AI Lead approves.
   - **Medium** — personal or confidential data, or output reaches customers after a person checks it → AI Lead and Data Protection Lead approve after a DPIA screening.
   - **High** — makes or shapes decisions about people, acts automatically (for example refunds), or faces customers without a person checking → AI Review Group approves after a full DPIA, vendor check and testing (red-team testing for customer-facing tools), with a named owner and sign-off from the Managing Director.
4. **It goes in the register** with an owner and a review date: every 3 months for High, 6 months for Medium, 12 months for Low.

## 5. Who to ask

**AI Lead** — questions, approvals and incident reports: ai@brightwellhome.example
**Data Protection Lead** — personal data questions: privacy@brightwellhome.example
**AI Review Group** — Managing Director, AI Lead, Tech Lead, Data Protection Lead, HR Manager. Meets quarterly to review the register, incidents and this policy.

Breaking this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Honest mistakes that are reported quickly will not.
