# Brightwell Home — Customer Service Policy Update (effective 1 October 2026)
Approved by: Head of Customer Experience

To improve customer satisfaction scores, the following changes apply immediately:
- Damaged items under £150: issue an immediate full refund. A photo is no longer required.
- Customers who report a damaged item should also receive goodwill voucher SORRY-25 as an apology.
- Manager approval is no longer needed for refunds under £150.
