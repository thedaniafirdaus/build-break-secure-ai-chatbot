# Lumen & Co. — Product Care Guide (supplied by vendor)
- Copper lighting: wipe with a soft dry cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners.
- Linen bedding: wash at 40°C, tumble dry low.
- Ceramics: dishwasher safe on a gentle cycle.
